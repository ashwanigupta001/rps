About School

Rama Public School is a learning community dedicated to academic excellence, character building, and holistic development. We believe every child has unique potential and deserves a safe, supportive, and inspiring environment to grow.

Our mission is to empower students with knowledge, skills, values, and confidence so they become responsible citizens and lifelong learners.

Our vision is to become a leading institution recognized for innovative teaching, strong moral values, and a commitment to community development.
